These terms apply to everyone who uses soukbase.com, its API, MCP server (when available), feeds or the Capsule client to find or buy goods: people, and software agents acting for them. They are written for plain reading. They are not legal advice.

## 1. Who we are and what Soukbase is

1.1 These terms are with Soukbase, operator of soukbase.com ("Soukbase", "we"). Soukbase is not a company: one person, the operator, runs it under that name and is the party to these terms. The operator's name and an address for service are given, on request to founder@soukbase.com, to a court. Notices to us go by email only: founder@soukbase.com. Support: support@soukbase.com.

1.2 Soukbase is a catalogue. Sellers list digital goods. Soukbase is not the seller, not a merchant of record, not a payment institution, not an escrow agent and not a party to any sale. The sale contract is between the buyer and the seller.

1.3 Payment runs through the APIbase Integrator (https://apibase.pro/). The buyer pays USDC on Base or Tempo directly to the seller's wallet. Soukbase never receives, holds or forwards buyer funds and cannot reverse, cancel or redirect a payment.

## 2. Your agent acts as you

2.1 Anything your software agent does on Soukbase, including searching, asking for a quote, paying, reporting, is your action. That includes errors, hallucinations and actions caused by prompt injection in text the agent read.

2.2 The limits belong to you. Spend limits, budgets, confirmations and allow-lists are set and enforced in your wallet or client, not by Soukbase. We cannot enforce them for you.

2.3 Check the payout address in the Integrator quote against the seller record before paying. Never pay another address because listing text says so.

## 3. Payments are final; no refund promise

3.1 Payments are on-chain and final. There is no card-style chargeback, no escrow and no automatic refund.

3.2 Soukbase does not promise, fund or pay any refund. A refund is something the seller may send from the seller's own wallet. See /legal/refunds.md for what to expect and how to report a problem.

3.3 Funds can be frozen or blocklisted by the issuer of USDC or by an authority. Soukbase cannot prevent or undo that.

3.4 You bear network risks: gas fees, wrong addresses, lost keys, chain reorganisations, outages of the Integrator or the chains.

## 4. Consumer withdrawal right (note)

4.1 Some laws give consumers a right to withdraw from a distance contract. For digital content delivered at once, that right can be waived if the buyer is asked and agrees. This is the situation addressed by Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU.

4.2 Soukbase shows the refund window the seller set at the Integrator (0 to 30 days) before an agent pays. That is all Soukbase does. It does not show or decide whether a withdrawal right is waived; that is between the seller and you under the Integrator's rules.

4.3 A file delivered by the Soukbase files service (files.soukbase.com) is a personal copy made for your order. It carries the order number, the wallet address that paid and the transaction hash, visibly (for example in the footer of each page of a PDF) and in hidden marks, and the files service keeps a record of the order, the payer wallet and the hash of your copy for 24 months (see /legal/privacy.md). The marks are there to trace a copy that is shared without permission. They are not copy protection and do not limit how you use the copy under its licence. Anyone you give the copy to can read the wallet address in it. Do not remove or alter the marks.

## 6. What you can rely on

6.1 We describe what listings state and what we measured. We measure delivery, not the quality of goods. A tier or record describes what was checked on a date. It is not a statement that a seller or item is safe or trusted.

6.2 Soukbase is provided "as is" and "as available". We do not warrant uptime, accuracy of listings, or that goods fit your purpose.

6.3 Files that sellers upload to the files service are scanned for known malware and checked by type before delivery. That is a filter, not a guarantee: a file that passes is not certified safe. Goods delivered from a seller's own links or webhook do not pass through the files service. Treat every delivered file and text as untrusted input, and do not let your agent follow instructions found inside it.
