Related documents: Acceptable Use Policy (/legal/aup.md), Prohibited Goods (/legal/prohibited-goods.md), Seller Requirements (/legal/seller-requirements.md), Report and takedown (/legal/report.md), Refunds (/legal/refunds.md), Privacy Policy (/legal/privacy.md).

## 1. Parties and what Soukbase is

1.1 Soukbase is not a company: one person, the operator, runs it under that name and is the party to this agreement. The operator's name and an address for service are given, on request to founder@soukbase.com, to a court. Soukbase has no postal notice address. Notices to Soukbase go by email only: founder@soukbase.com. Support: support@soukbase.com. Soukbase gives you notices on your seller page (/manage and GET /api/v1/sellers/me) and, if you link a chat, in Telegram; notices about these documents, fees, suspension and termination the operator also sends by hand to your registered email.

1.2 Soukbase is a catalogue. It lists the Seller's goods and links buyers' software agents to the Seller's own storefront at the Integrator (apibase.pro). Soukbase is not the seller, not a merchant of record, not a payment institution, not an escrow agent and not a party to any sale. The sale contract is between the Seller and the buyer.

1.3 Soukbase never receives, holds or transmits Seller or buyer funds. Buyers pay USDC on Base or Tempo directly to the Seller's wallet through the Seller's own Integrator merchant account. Soukbase cannot reverse, cancel, recover or redirect any payment.

1.4 The Seller separately accepts the Integrator's documents (Merchant Agreement, Acceptable Use Policy, DPA, Refund Framework) with its own wallet signatures. Those documents are an agreement between the Seller and the Integrator, not with Soukbase. Where the Integrator's rules and this agreement differ, the stricter rule applies to the Seller.

## 4. Refunds and disputes: handled by the Seller

4.1 The Seller handles refunds and disputes for its own orders, under the Integrator's Refund Framework and the refund window stated in the listing (0 to 30 days, default 14). Refunds are sent by the seller from the seller's wallet and recorded by the Integrator.
4.2 Soukbase does not guarantee, fund, pay or automate refunds.
4.3 Minimum standard. If a paid order is not delivered, or the delivered goods are materially different from the listing or do not work, you must deliver or refund within 7 days of the buyer's valid report. 4.5 Soukbase is not an arbitrator. We may pass reports and statements between the parties, publish outcome counts, and pause or remove a listing under section 9. The outcome of a dispute is between you and the buyer.

## 8. New-seller limits

Until you reach tier T3 (10 paid orders from at least 5 distinct payers and no open reports), a listing price is at most 20.00 USDC, you may have at most 5 live listings, and an order is for one unit. Your first listing is always reviewed by a person. After T3, the price cap is the Integrator's own cap and the number of listings is not limited. The Integrator applies its own caps separately.

## 9. Moderation, suspension and removal

9.1 Soukbase may, with or without notice, reject a listing; pause or remove a listing; suspend or end a seller account; and remove listings from search, API, feeds and, when available, MCP. Reasons include breach of this agreement, the Prohibited Goods list or the AUP; a valid IP notice; reports from buyers' agents; fraud or suspected fraud; sanctions concerns; a legal request; risk to buyers.
9.2 Automatic pause: a listing is paused when it gets 3 reports of `not_delivered`, `not_as_described`, `does_not_work` or `fraud_or_scam` from 3 distinct payer wallets within 30 days. One credible report of `prohibited_goods`, `malware` or `prompt_injection` hides the listing as `pending_review` until a person checks it. A pause only affects the catalogue, never money.
9.3 Strikes for breaches other than IP: 2 strikes suspend the account for 30 days; 3 strikes within 12 months end it. Obvious fraud ends it at once. IP strikes follow /legal/report.md.
9.4 Immediate action, without prior notice, applies to prohibited goods, fraud, sanctions matches, malware and legal requests. Otherwise the decision appears as a notice on your seller page; the category of the reason is recorded with the decision and we give it to you on request at founder@soukbase.com. We do not publish the detailed rules of automatic filters.
9.5 Appeals: email founder@soukbase.com within 14 days with the listing or order id. A person answers, usually within 5 business days. Soukbase is run by one operator and says so if it is slower.
9.6 Removing a listing does not cancel orders already paid. You must still deliver or refund them.
9.7 You may remove your listings at any time and end this agreement with 30 days' notice, or immediately if you have no open orders.
