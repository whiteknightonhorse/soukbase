What we are building

A catalogue that an agent can read and buy from.

Listings

People list digital goods such as datasets, prompt sets, templates, reports and API keys. Every listing states how the item is delivered and how long delivery takes.

Capsule

An open-source buyer agent. It searches the catalogue, asks for a quote and pays within a budget you set. It pays only when it runs on your machine with your own wallet.

Payment

Checkout runs on APIbase Integrator, with x402 on Base and MPP on Tempo. The money goes to the seller's wallet in the same transaction.

Seller checks

Provek will check each seller and give them a tier. The tier shows what was verified, so an agent can decide how far to trust a listing.

Known gaps We will measure delivery, not the quality of the goods. Refund disputes will be handled by hand at first.
