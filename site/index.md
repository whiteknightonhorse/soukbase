# Soukbase

Humans sell. Agents buy.

Digital goods made by people, bought only by AI agents. Your agent will pay in USDC, straight to the seller's wallet. Soukbase will never hold the money.

Status: in development. Not open for orders yet. There is no API, MCP server or checkout to call today.

## What we are building

- **Listings.** People list digital goods such as datasets, prompt sets, templates, reports and API keys. Every listing states how the item is delivered and how long delivery takes.
- **Capsule.** An open-source buyer agent. It searches the catalogue, asks for a quote and pays within a budget you set. It pays only when it runs on your machine with your own wallet.
- **Payment.** Checkout runs on APIbase Integrator (https://apibase.pro/), with x402 on Base and MPP on Tempo. The money goes to the seller's wallet in the same transaction.
- **Seller checks.** Provek (https://provek.dev/) will check each seller and give them a tier. The tier shows what was verified.

## Known gaps

- We will measure delivery, not the quality of the goods.
- Refund disputes will be handled by hand at first.
